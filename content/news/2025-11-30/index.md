Summary: November focused on further content enrichment, improved validation workflows, and enhanced user experience through illustration and story updates. The month saw a continued commitment to quality, automation, and accessibility across the platform.

Summary of changes

- Content enrichment: Additional illustrations were added to popular stories, especially in the "Trang Quynh" collection, making the reading experience more engaging and visually appealing.

- Validation and automation: The validation workflow was refined, with improved reporting and artifact uploads. Automated checks now better detect missing or incorrect frontmatter, helping maintain high content quality and reducing manual review effort.

- Metadata and path standardization: Ongoing standardization of story paths and metadata fields (such as genres and origins) ensures a more consistent browsing experience and reliable navigation throughout the site.

- UI/UX improvements: Story cards, thumbnails, and illustration displays were further polished for consistency. These changes enhance the overall look and feel, especially on mobile devices, and ensure that featured stories and new content are highlighted effectively.

- Build and workflow reliability: Automation scripts and CI processes were updated to minimize errors and streamline content deployment, resulting in a smoother editorial workflow and more stable site updates.

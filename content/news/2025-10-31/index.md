Summary: October was a month of large-scale content additions, metadata standardization, and automation improvements. The repository saw steady content growth (especially stories and illustrations for Vietnam, Thailand, and spiritual collections), wide-ranging path and frontmatter standardization, and the introduction of CI-assisted validation artifacts to catch frontmatter issues automatically.

Summary of changes (functional & UX-focused)

- Content expansion: Large batches of new stories and illustration assets were added across multiple regions, increasing the site's breadth and the number of illustrated story pages available to readers.

- Metadata normalization: The content model and frontmatter fields (genres, reading times, age groups, origins, and path conventions) were standardized across the repository to enforce consistent data shapes and predictable URLs.

- Validation and automation: CI validation and artifact uploads were introduced or improved to automatically detect missing or malformed frontmatter and to provide machine-readable reports for quick triage.

- UX polish and media handling: Illustration prompts and asset placement were standardized so images and WebP conversions are more consistently available; cards, thumbnails, and reading-time displays should now render more uniformly across story listing pages.

- Build & workflow stability: Small fixes in automation and file handling reduced noisy failures and improved reliability of content pipelines (fewer spurious build/validation errors and more stable uploads of generated artifacts).

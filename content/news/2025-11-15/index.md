We’ve added a new Refresh Menu feature to the site's top menu.

![](image.png)

The menu shall refresh automatically every 5 minutes, giving readers new content to explore.

The menu are drawn randomly from our list of:
- [Genres](https://tellstory.net/genres/)
- [Themes](https://tellstory.net/themes/)
- [Origins](https://tellstory.net/origins/)

to ensure a diverse and broad contents each refresh

Can't wait? You can click (or tap) on the ⟳ icon to force an immediate refresh.

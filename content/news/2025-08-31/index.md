**Summary of Changes:**

- Added and standardized Bible stories, Korean fairy tales, Japanese fairy tales, Zulu folklore, and Arabian tales.
- Improved illustration handling, added missing figures, and cleaned up illustration files.
- Major theme consolidation: reduced themes to canonical set, demoted specifics to tags, and regenerated analysis reports.
- Enhanced social integration: added share buttons, improved social icons, and enabled posting to Instagram, Threads, and X.
- Improved taxonomy analysis and standardization scripts for genres, themes, origins, age groups, and reading times.
- Various UI/UX improvements: meta tag display, share button styling, max height for images, and toggle opacity.
- Maintenance: updated ignore files, cleaned up scripts, and synced repository state.

**Expected Outcome:**

- A more organized, visually appealing, and socially integrated site.
- Easier taxonomy management and improved content discoverability.
- Better illustration coverage and performance.

**Evaluation:**

- August saw significant progress in content, taxonomy, and social features. The site is now more robust, user-friendly, and ready for further growth.
